# B2B Growth Funnel & Marketing Efficiency Analytics

## Business Context

A B2B company acquires potential customers through multiple marketing
channels including Google Ads, LinkedIn, Email, Organic Search,
Referrals and Webinars.

Although the company generates a steady volume of leads, leadership
does not have a clear understanding of which acquisition channels,
campaigns and customer segments generate qualified opportunities and
revenue.

The company also lacks visibility into where potential customers drop
out of the Lead → MQL → SQL → Customer funnel and how long successful
customers take to progress through the sales process.

## Business Objective

Build an end-to-end analytics solution that helps Marketing, Sales and
Revenue Operations understand acquisition efficiency, funnel
performance, sales velocity and revenue contribution.

## Stakeholders

### Head of Marketing
Needs to understand which channels and campaigns generate efficient
customer acquisition.

### Sales Manager
Needs visibility into funnel leakage and conversion performance.

### Revenue Operations
Needs consistent metrics for acquisition cost, conversion, funnel
velocity and revenue.

### Leadership
Needs a high-level view of marketing investment and resulting revenue.

## Core Business Questions

1. Which marketing channels generate the highest-quality leads?
2. Which campaigns generate the strongest customer conversion?
3. Where is the largest funnel drop-off?
4. Which channels have the lowest customer acquisition cost?
5. Which channels and campaigns generate the strongest ROAS?
6. Which industries, regions and company sizes convert most effectively?
7. How long does it take leads to move through each sales stage?
8. Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
9. Are monthly acquisition cohorts improving?
10. Which campaigns should be scaled, optimized or investigated?

## Primary KPIs

- Total Leads
- MQLs
- SQLs
- Customers
- Lead-to-MQL Conversion Rate
- MQL-to-SQL Conversion Rate
- SQL-to-Customer Conversion Rate
- Overall Lead-to-Customer Conversion Rate
- Acquisition Spend
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Revenue
- Revenue per Lead
- ROAS
- Average Sales Cycle
- Funnel Drop-off Rate
